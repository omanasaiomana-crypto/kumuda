First Bench, Second Bench & A Surprise Hair Pull

We met in 2020, after COVID. I was sitting on the first bench and Kumuda was sitting right behind me on the second bench. forumChapter III • “Let's Sit Together and Study.”
2022

5 Mins Later: Talking. 10 Mins Later: Still Talking. 😂

During tests and exams, we would make a serious plan to sit together and study. But somehow... 5 minutes later: talking. 10 minutes later: still talking. Eventually: zero studying, maximum conversation. 😂 Every teacher knew us so well that they would sometimes say my name when they meant Kumuda and Kumuda's name when they meant me. At that point, even our teachers knew we were basically a package deal.

Bangalore Distance & Happy Birthday Kumuda! 🎉

Then Kumuda moved to Bangalore. Suddenly, my second-bench partner wasn't sitting behind me anymore. I really, really missed her. Distance couldn't dim what began with a hair pull. Happy Birthday Kumuda!
