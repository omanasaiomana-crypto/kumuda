## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, commemorative, and reverent digital space celebrating a monumental life milestone: a dearest friend becoming a physician. The platform blends the warmth and intimacy of a cherished keepsake scrapbook with the quiet precision and nobility of the medical profession.

The design movement combines **Editorial Warm Glassmorphism** with **Tactile Keepsake Storytelling**. Frosted parchment surfaces, glowing amber lights, deep celebratory crimson drapes, and hairline golden accents intermingle with subtle clinical touches—such as delicate pulsewave vectors transformed into golden timelines, fine-lined anatomical botanical flourishes, and embossed foil seals.

The emotional resonance is celebratory, deeply nostalgic, proud, and timeless. The audience includes lifelong friends, mentors, family members, and medical peers contributing reflections, voice memos, photo polaroids, and congratulations.

## Colors

The color palette is pulled directly from the atmospheric depth and regality of the tribute portrait:

- **Primary (`#8B1E2F`) — Royal Crimson / Deep Bordeaux**: Drawn directly from the rich, gold-embroidered silk skirt in the photograph. Represents passion, clinical heartbeats, deep dedication, and celebratory triumph.
- **Secondary (`#D4A359`) — Antique Luminescent Gold**: Sourced from the warm ambient lantern glow, fairy lights, and embroidered brocade. Used for key achievements, badges, milestones, borders, and interactive celebratory states.
- **Tertiary (`#2C4A3E`) — Deep Eucalyptus & Scrub Spruce**: A poised, grounded medical pine green providing balanced contrast against golden-red hues; utilized for MD verification marks, clinical dates, and subtle accent indicators.
- **Neutral (`#241819`) — Warm Ebony Velvet**: A rich, ink-toned brown-black with warm undertones, avoiding cold synthetic grays to preserve intimacy.
- **Canvas Base & Surface Containers**: Warm clotted cream (`#FAF6F0`), aged parchment alabaster (`#F4EDE2`), and frosted glass sheets tinted with 8% amber warmth (`rgba(255, 250, 242, 0.72)`).

## Typography

Typography balances high-fashion editorial distinction with empathetic accessibility:

- **Headlines & Expressive Titles**: Set in **Playfair Display**, evoking classic academic graduation honours, traditional medical treatises, and refined personal correspondence. It is treated with slightly tightened tracking and soft italicization for personal quotes.
- **Body & Longform Memory Narratives**: Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering open counters and friendly humanist curves. This provides fatigue-free reading across nostalgic journal excerpts, memory captions, and heartfelt notes.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set in uppercase and medium/semi-bold **Plus Jakarta Sans** with expanded letter spacing, serving as elegant cataloging cues (e.g., "YEAR 2018: PRE-MED DAYS", "ANATOMY ROTATIONS", "NOTE #142").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, rhythmic **12-column fluid grid system** (max-width `1280px` for desktop) anchored by generous negative space that simulates the breathing room of an artisanal art book:

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` side margins. Asymmetrical card alignments allow childhood photos and medical school milestone certificates to overlap dynamically with subtle depth.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (up to 767px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Cards snap into linear storytelling stacks with gentle horizontal scrolling carousels for the photo album filmstrips.
- **Rhythm & Safe Margin**: Spacing scales are based on a 4px/8px structural cadence, where `space-2xl` is strictly utilized between thematic milestones (e.g., transition from "School Days" to "MBBS & Residency").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy evokes the tangible stacking of thick paper mementos, frosted glass lantern displays, and luminous warm backlight:

- **Surface 0 (Base)**: Warm clotted cream and parchment foundation with an imperceptible 3% organic noise texture to prevent digital sterility.
- **Surface 1 (Scrapbook Slips & Timeline Tracks)**: Slightly lifted parchment planes bordered with a soft hairline `rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.25)` gold edge and a warm diffuse amber shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(139, 30, 47, 0.04)`).
- **Surface 2 (Glassmorphism Memory Cards & Guestbook Submissions)**: Frosted translucent containers (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `background: rgba(255, 252, 247, 0.75)`), accented with dual rim highlights: a `1px` inner top border of pure light (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) and an exterior cast shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 24, 25, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.12)`).
- **Surface 3 (Overlays, Modal Envelopes, Lightbox Previews)**: High-altitude elements with backdrop dimming in deep bordeaux-tinted charcoal (`rgba(36, 24, 25, 0.65)` with `8px` blur) and an elevated shadow (`0 24px 64px -8px rgba(36, 24, 25, 0.22)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature with specialized contextual nuances:

- Standard interactive cards, guestbook cards, and text containers feature `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) corners, simulating fine, die-cut stationery and rounded polaroid corners.
- Interactive tags, audio pills (voice messages), and congratulatory chips utilize full pill contours (`9999px`).
- Memory image frames balance rounded soft corners with occasional subtle decorative gold corner brackets or postal-stamp perforated borders.
- Medical motif elements—such as pulse heartbeat paths and stethoscope curved lines—act as continuous smooth vector ribbons weaving between sections.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Celebrate / Leave a Note")**: Rich crimson background (`#8B1E2F`) transitioning on hover to deep bordeaux (`#721825`), featuring crisp white serif or semibold sans typography, paired with a delicate gold focus ring (`#D4A359`). Elevation rises by 2px on interaction.
- **Secondary / Ghost ("View Medical Journey")**: Warm transparent glass with a hairline antique gold border (`1.5px solid #D4A359`), text in crimson or warm dark ebony, acquiring a subtle golden frosted glow on hover.
- **Pill Icon Buttons**: Round buttons housing celebratory confetti triggers, bookmark pins, and audio playback toggles for spoken tributes.

### Cards & Memory Polaroids
- **Glass Tribute Card**: Houses heartfelt quotes and photos with a semi-opaque parchment fill (`rgba(255, 250, 242, 0.8)`), soft `16px` blur, gold corner detailing, and an author attribution block.
- **Polaroid Keepsake**: Features an asymmetric top/bottom white border mimicking vintage film with the handwritten-style font option for captions, tilted at natural ±1.5° angles on desktop hover.

### Timeline Track (The Journey from 8th Standard to MD)
- A flowing golden-threaded lifeline combining an EKG/heartbeat wave with milestone nodes. Completed stages (e.g., "Board Exams", "NEET/MCAT Crack", "White Coat Ceremony", "MD Residency") are symbolized by golden wax-seal icons and caduceus crests that unfold into contextual drawers when clicked.

### Congratulatory Guestbook & Input Fields
- **Input Fields & Letter Slips**: Textured warm paper styling with faint baseline guides mimicking vintage stationery. Focus state transitions from faint gold border to an assertive `#8B1E2F` border accompanied by an ambient warm gold inner shadow.
- **Interactive Stamp Chips**: Filter chips enabling guests to sort messages ("Memories", "High School Days", "Med School Tears & Joy", "Proud Family").

### Medical & Keepsake Specialty Accents
- **Stethoscope Golden Divider**: Decorative horizontal rule that dips in the center into an elegant minimalist heart or caduceus knot.
- **Audio Message Player**: Waveform visualized through rhythmic pulse lines bathed in amber gold, permitting loved ones to listen to voice notes recorded for Dr. Friend.
